Puerto Rico · December 2025 to present

What happens when you finally move back?

For almost 19 years, Puerto Rico was a place I came back to visit. In December 2025, it became my home again.

Now I get to enjoy the things I used to miss from a distance: the food, the music, the familiar expressions, and the freedom to spend an ordinary afternoon somewhere I once had to fly to visit. But there is a lot more to moving back than booking a one-way ticket.

Here I'll be sharing the places I explore, the things I discover, and what it takes to build a life here after so many years away.
